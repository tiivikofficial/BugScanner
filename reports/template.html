5. Methodology & Confidence

Findings are deduplicated and enriched with deterministic evidence/confidence metadata before risk prioritization. Confidence is based on collected evidence, payload presence, reproduction material, exploitation evidence, parameter context, references and confirmation count. Automated findings should be manually verified before external disclosure.

6. Limitations

  • This report reflects only the assets and endpoints reached during this scan.
  • A scanner finding is not, by itself, proof of exploitability or business impact.
  • Missing evidence or low confidence should be treated as a verification lead, not a confirmed vulnerability.
  • External tools that were unavailable during the run are not represented as successful tests.
